*Methodology*

Quality ratings prioritize large-repository understanding, complex debugging, code review, long-horizon engineering, and C/C++ correctness. SWE-bench, LiveCodeBench, and other SWE evaluations also inform the qualitative assessment where comparable data exists. The main table uses independent results; where none exists, it shows another independent run (`‡`) or a vendor-reported result (`†`).

It ties Claude Opus 5.5 for first place on Vals AI Terminal-Bench 4.0 (65.2%), leads Artificial Analysis FrontierCode 2.0 (49.1% vs 46.4% for Opus 5.5), and is the best Claude model on CursorBench 4.0 (60.0%, second only to Gemini 4 Carbon). *Gemini 4 Carbon* ranks #4, tied with GPT-6 Astra. It leads CursorBench 4.0 (60.4%) and scores 63.1% on Vals AI Terminal-Bench 4.0 and 61.2% on FrontierSWE. *Recommendation for Percona Server for MySQL*

Top candidates for a controlled head-to-head evaluation are *Claude Fable 5.2, Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Astra, and Gemini 4 Carbon*. Fable 5.2 is the strongest model overall, but it costs twice as much as Opus 5.5; compare Fable 5.2 at high effort against Opus 5.5 at max effort. Gemini 4 Carbon is cheap to trial at its introductory price until November 30. Kimi K3.1 Pro remains worth a trial for long-running terminal and agentic tasks. Test them against real InnoDB concurrency issues, replication bugs, crash recovery, memory-safety problems, complex code reviews, and MTR regression tests.
