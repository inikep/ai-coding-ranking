*Recommendation for Percona Server for MySQL*

Top candidates for a controlled head-to-head evaluation are *Claude Fable 5.2, Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6.2 Astra, and Gemini 4 Carbon*. Fable 5.2 is still the strongest model overall and also leads both C/C++ columns (SWE Atlas and ports into C++); compare it at high effort against Opus 5.5 at max effort. GPT-6.2 Astra now costs the same as Fable 5.2 and matches it on FrontierCode 2.0, but early reports of stopping early and being hard to steer make it a better candidate for code review than for long autonomous tasks until DeepSWE and FrontierSWE results arrive. Trial Gemini 4 Carbon before its introductory price is reported to end on November 30. Kimi K3.1 Pro remains worth a trial for long-running terminal and agentic tasks. For high-volume work such as test-failure triage and first-pass code review, Gemini 4 Hydrogen, GPT-6.1 Terra and Claude Haiku 5.5 are the cheaper options. Where C++ quality matters more than price, Claude Sonnet 5.5 ($2 / $10) scores 84.4% on ports into C++, far ahead of the cheapest models with a result (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 55.5%, Claude Haiku 5.5 41.2%, GLM 5.3 26.9%). Test them against real InnoDB concurrency issues, replication bugs, crash recovery, memory-safety problems, complex code reviews, and MTR regression tests.
