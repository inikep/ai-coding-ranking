What changed since November 13

  • GPT-6.2 Astra (OpenAI, Nov 17) replaces GPT-6 Astra at #3. Independent results so far beat GPT-6 Astra: 63.1% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (was 59.6%) and 49.0% on FrontierCode 2.0, 0.1 points behind Claude Fable 5.2.   • The list grows to 20: GPT-6.1 Terra, Claude Haiku 5.5, Muse Spark 1.3 and Muse Ember join. Smaller tiers are now ranked on merit.

Recommendation

For a controlled head-to-head evaluation, start with Claude Fable 5.2, Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6.2 Astra and Gemini 4 Carbon. Fable 5.2 is still the strongest model overall; compare it at high effort against Opus 5.5 at max effort. GPT-6.2 Astra matches Fable 5.2 on FrontierCode 2.0 at the same price, but until DeepSWE and FrontierSWE results arrive it is a better fit for code review than for long autonomous tasks. Trial Gemini 4 Carbon before its introductory price ends. Kimi K3.1 Pro remains worth a trial for long terminal and agentic tasks. For high-volume work such as test-failure triage and first-pass review, Gemini 4 Hydrogen, GPT-6.1 Terra and Claude Haiku 5.5 are the cheaper options. Test them on real InnoDB concurrency issues, replication bugs, crash recovery, memory-safety problems, complex code reviews and MTR regression tests.

Method

Ratings weigh large-repository understanding, complex debugging, code review, long-horizon engineering and C/C++ correctness. They are estimates, not a formula over the benchmarks: benchmarks use different agent harnesses and reasoning settings, and the table shows each model's best published configuration. Independent results are preferred; vendor results appear only where nothing else exists and are marked †. Prices are indicative; where OpenRouter lists several rate tiers, the vendor's standard tier is shown.
