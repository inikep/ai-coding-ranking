*Methodology*

Quality ratings prioritize large-repository understanding, complex debugging, code review, long-horizon engineering, and C/C++ correctness. SWE-bench, LiveCodeBench, and other SWE evaluations also inform the qualitative assessment where comparable data exists. The main table uses independent results; where none exists, it shows another independent run (`‡`) or a vendor-reported result (`†`).

*Recommendation for Percona Server for MySQL*

Top candidates for a controlled head-to-head evaluation are *Claude Fable 5.2, Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Astra, and Gemini 4 Carbon*. Fable 5.2 is the strongest model overall, but it costs twice as much as Opus 5.5; compare Fable 5.2 at high effort against Opus 5.5 at max effort. Gemini 4 Carbon is cheap to trial at its introductory price until November 30. Kimi K3.1 Pro remains worth a trial for long-running terminal and agentic tasks. Gemini 4 Hydrogen is a cheaper option for high-volume work such as test-failure triage and first-pass code review. Test them against real InnoDB concurrency issues, replication bugs, crash recovery, memory-safety problems, complex code reviews, and MTR regression tests.
